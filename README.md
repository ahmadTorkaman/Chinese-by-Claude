# 道德經 Writing Practice App

## Installation Guide for iPad

### Step 1: Get the Files to Your iPad

**Option A: Via iCloud Drive**
1. Download the `daodejing-pwa` folder
2. Upload it to your iCloud Drive
3. Access it via the Files app on iPad

**Option B: Via a Simple Web Server**
If you have a computer on your local network:
```bash
cd daodejing-pwa
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
Then open Safari on your iPad and go to: `http://YOUR_COMPUTER_IP:8000`

**Option C: Host Online (Recommended)**
Upload the folder to any free hosting service:
- GitHub Pages (free)
- Netlify (free)
- Vercel (free)

### Step 2: Add to Home Screen

1. **Open Safari** on your iPad (must be Safari, not Chrome)
2. Navigate to the app URL
3. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow pointing up)
4. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Name it "道德經" or "Dao De Jing"
6. Tap **"Add"**

### Step 3: Launch the App

The app will now appear on your home screen with the 道 icon. Tap it to launch in full-screen mode!

## Features

### 📖 Study Mode
- View classical Chinese, modern Chinese, and English translations
- Tap any character to see its meaning and hear pronunciation
- Navigate through lines and chapters

### ✍️ Practice Mode

**Stroke Order Tab:**
- Watch animated stroke order for each character
- Press "Animate" to see the stroke sequence
- Press "Quiz Me" to practice drawing with feedback

**Free Write Tab:**
- Draw characters with your Apple Pencil
- Ghost character guides your strokes
- Grid lines help with proportions

### 🔊 Audio
- Tap "Listen" to hear the full line
- Tap "Play Sound" to hear individual characters
- Uses your device's Chinese text-to-speech

## Chapters Included

1. **Chapter 1: The Way (道)** - The opening verses about the ineffable Dao
2. **Chapter 8: Water (水)** - The famous "highest good is like water" passage  
3. **Chapter 81: True Words (信言)** - The closing chapter on truth and wisdom

---

## Tips for Learning

1. **Start with Chapter 8** - It's shorter and has common characters
2. **Practice stroke order first** - Watch the animation multiple times
3. **Write each character 10 times** - Repetition builds muscle memory
4. **Say the pinyin aloud** - Connect sound to writing
5. **Review daily** - Short consistent practice beats long sporadic sessions

---

## Offline Use

Once you've loaded the app and added it to your home screen, it will work offline! The service worker caches all necessary files.
